**Fetching Reality: Bring a Static Dog Photo to Life in Real-Time**

**Overview**  
Blending art, computer graphics, mathematics, and human-computer interaction (HCI), this project tasks you with bringing a static dog photo to life. Your goal is to build a real-time interactive application where this dog moves naturally within the screen space and interacts with users just like a real-world pet.

Creativity beyond imagination: No only just image or photoshop the layer on top or generative AI video, turn the images into dynamic interactable with you, bring the pet to life in your screen.

**The Challenge**  
Think outside the box! Go beyond standard behaviors like sitting, begging for treats, or wagging a tail. We want to see dynamic interactions—like playing fetch within the screen space, environmental awareness, or creative digital tricks.

To push the boundaries of modern graphics, we encourage you to move beyond traditional 3D mesh-based rendering. Explore advanced representations like Signed Distance Fields (SDFs), 3D Gaussian Splatting (3GS), or other novel techniques. We highly encourage participants to leverage AI-powered tools alongside standard computer graphics pipelines, mathematical algorithms, and HCI design principles to pull this off.  

**Judging Criteria**  
Submissions will be evaluated based on three core pillars:

1. **Computational Efficiency**: Performance, framerate, and optimization for real-time execution.  
2. **Interactivity**: How responsive, engaging, and seamless the human-dog interaction feels.  
3. **Aesthetics & Animation**: The visual quality of the reconstructed dog and the fluidity of its movements.
